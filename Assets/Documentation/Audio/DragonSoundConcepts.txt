Soul Eater / Dragon - replacement audio proposal
Created: 2026-10-08

29 individual sounds and seven audition sequences.
Voice A: deeper, animal layered growl. Voice B: raspier, hissing dragon.
Each voice has a 2.55 s phase roar, 1.60 s short roar, hurt and death.
Common effects: bite preparation/execution, tail preparation/execution,
inhalation, breath start/loop/end, ground fire, one wingbeat/wingbeat loop,
takeoff, dive wind, two footsteps, landing, dive impact, charge preparation,
charge start/running loop, and braking.

Status: listening proposal; no live clips, runtime scripts, prefabs, scenes,
or settings are changed. Old SoulEater_*.wav retain their original content
and GUIDs. New clips are in Assets/Art/Audio/Enemies/SoulEater/Concept.
Preview: output/dragon-audio/escuchar.html.

Known integration work after selection:
- SoulEaterEffects currently has seven indexed cues plus one flame loop.
- Bite and Tail cues currently fire at windup; split preparation and execution
  instead of firing an impact early. Preserve the controller's timing/damage.
- Distinguish DustImpact Charge, Landing and Dive; they currently share Land.
- Add loop lifecycle for wings, running and ground fire; stop or pause on
  state transitions, interruption, pause, disable and death.
- Use the short roar for the 1.65 s special roar and long roar for the 2.6 s
  phase transition. Inhale is 1.38 s within the 1.5 s breath windup.
- Breath start and tail should follow actual emission. Airborne and grounded
  breath can share the steady loop, which must end on interruption.
- Add voice hurt/death through confirmed combat results, with hurt cooldown.
- Existing SFX mixer and spatial audio must apply; avoid another hit stop or
  camera shaker. Wire arrival presentation audio as well as combat audio.
- Verify these additions in Unity before calling the sound set integrated.

Generator: Assets/Scripts/Audio/Editor/GenerateDragonSoundConcepts.py
Requires NumPy and SoundFile. Local SoundFile fallback: .validation/audio-python.
Export: mono 48 kHz PCM16; source resampling does not restore lost bandwidth.
Loops use a continuous seam, not a fade-to-silence gap. Auditions apply short
entry/exit ramps to loop layers. Audio-analysis.json records file hashes,
durations and levels. No in-game timing or content build verified yet.
