The original 24-bit / 48 kHz mono recordings are preserved, unmodified, in:
Assets/Art/Audio/Enemies/Imp/Source
Their archive and individual SHA256 hashes are in ImpVoiceConcepts-source.json.

Derivatives: Assets/Art/Audio/Enemies/Imp/Concept
Changes: crops at silence boundaries, small pitch/varispeed shifts, EQ,
parallel saturation, level matching, edge fades and 16-bit PCM export.
Three timbre profiles are reproducible. Profile B (Balanced) was selected for
attack and hit; the listening page now focuses on that profile.
The preparation was rejected in all three original profiles and replaced by
a different recorded gesture, cropped to 0.44 s before varispeed, with an
80 ms tail fade and 0.78 gain. Attack and hit rendering is unchanged.

Approved game uses:
Prepare <- SmallCreature3_17.wav (0.005–0.445 s), approved replacement
Attack  <- SmallCreature3_09.wav (0.015–0.535 s)
Hit     <- SmallCreature3_10.wav (0.015–0.555 s)
These role labels are Mismo's proposals, not descriptions supplied by the actor.
SmallCreature3_15.wav is preserved as an unused source from the rejected take.

These are sound-editing auditions, not newly recorded or AI-generated acting.
AAA Game Character Hellspawn by Epic Stock Media informed the requested
direction only. No audio from the commercial pack or its demo was used.
The three approved Balanced WAVs are referenced directly by Imp settings:
preparation and execution of the melee action, and the confirmed-hit voice.
Their files and GUIDs are preserved. The other timbre auditions remain unused.

FIRE AUDIO INTEGRATION

Assets/Art/Audio/Enemies/Imp/Fire contains three original procedural effects:
Imp_Fire_Prepare.wav: 1.18 s rising flame/crackle with the approved preparation.
Imp_Fire_Launch.wav: 0.64 s fire burst/whoosh with the approved attack voice.
Imp_Fire_Impact.wav: 1.25 s spreading flame and crackling embers, without a voice.
The first two are derivatives of the credited voice recordings above.
Fire textures are deterministic filtered noise and granular crackles generated
for Mismo; no third-party fire audio is used. The revised impact replaces the
rejected pressure pulse/pop with rounded ignition and sustained turbulent fire.

The fireball action references these as preparation, execution and projectile
impact respectively. Its existing 1.2 s windup, projectile speed, VFX, ground
pool, damage and cooldowns are unchanged. The impact plays at the collision
position through the existing spatial SFX mixer route.

Rebuild: python Assets/Scripts/Audio/Editor/GenerateImpVoiceConcepts.py
Dependency: NumPy.
Then run: python Assets/Scripts/Audio/Editor/GenerateImpFireSounds.py
This preserves the approved B WAVs and refreshes the integrated listening page.
Unity setup menu: Mismo > Audio > Integrar voz B y fuego del Imp
Unity validation: ImpAudioIntegration.RunBatch (dedicated batch process).
