SPIDER SOUND CONCEPTS — MISMO

Profile B (Hissing) is selected and assigned to Spider settings.
Profile A (Dry), emphasizing chattering, remains as an unused audition.
Each has bite preparation/execution, jump preparation/execution and received hit.
The existing active actions have windups of 0.48 s (bite) and 0.60 s (jump).
The web-spitting action is disabled and has not been changed.

Changes for Mismo: stereo-to-mono mix, resampling to 48 kHz, selected short crops,
varispeed/pitch changes, frequency filtering, layering, fades, level matching,
and PCM16 export. The jump includes an original filtered-noise air gesture.
No audio from the commercial Spider Vocalizations demo was downloaded or used.

Generator: Assets/Scripts/Audio/Editor/GenerateSpiderSoundConcepts.py (NumPy).
Outputs: Assets/Art/Audio/Enemies/Spider/Concept.
Listening page: output/spider-audio/escuchar.html.
The A/B reels follow bite preparation, bite, jump preparation, jump and hit.

GAME INTEGRATION

Assets/Data/Enemies/ForestCreatures/Spider.asset references the five Hissing
clips directly. Their WAV bytes and metadata are preserved. Preparation and
execution use volume 0.8; received hit uses 0.75, through the existing SFX route.
The Standard, Forest Moss, Dark Cave and Albino prefabs share these settings.
Attack timings, damage, animations and the disabled web action are preserved.

Unity setup menu: Mismo > Audio > Integrar sonidos B de la araña.
SpiderAudioIntegration.RunBatch checks the four actual prefabs, a Windows
content bundle and attack/hurt/cancellation events in a temporary Play scene.
Reports: output/spider-audio/integration-checks.txt and play-checks.txt.
Content-bundle validation does not replace a full player build.
