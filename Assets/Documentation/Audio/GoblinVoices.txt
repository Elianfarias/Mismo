Edits: silence trimming, mono conversion, approximately one semitone of
downward varispeed, gentle EQ/saturation, edge fades, headroom matching,
48 kHz / 16-bit PCM export. Preparations contain quiet original procedural
Foley layers; hurt voices use recorded vocals only. Both attack executions
use the approved B effort, about 0.262 seconds, peak 0.52, without movement
or impact layers. Their WAV bytes match output/goblin-audio/effort-options/
Goblin_Esfuerzo_B.wav. The goblin-9.wav source remains archived and unused.

Rebuild: python Assets/Scripts/Audio/Editor/GenerateGoblinSounds.py
Requires NumPy. Preserves the seven existing Unity GUIDs and references.

Goblin Vocalizations by Magic Sound Effects was a direction reference only.
No audio from its commercial pack or public demo was copied or incorporated.
