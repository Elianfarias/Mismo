Mismo - Boar sound concepts
Created: 2026-10-08

Two listening profiles; B with the revised animal hit is integrated:
A / Woodland: short dry grunts, a warning snort and a brief hurt squeal.
B / Bristled: more body and rasp, with a stronger snort at charge launch.
B is the preferred profile. Its revised Hit uses only the real pig recording
Pig_Grunt_CC0.mp3 (0.965-1.255 s, 1.18x speed, filters and fades), lasting
0.29 s. The previous toy squeal and performed voice are absent from this hit.
The other four B combat sounds and all A sounds are unchanged.
Original Pig_Hit2.mp3 is retained for provenance but no longer used.
Each contains Attack_Prepare, Attack_Execute, Charge_Prepare, Charge_Execute
and Hit. Audition files combine these gestures using the existing 0.55 s
melee windup and 0.72 s charge windup. Execution belongs at attack launch.

level matching, and layering. Derived mixes distributed under CC BY 3.0;

The source manifest contains exact download URLs and SHA256 checksums.

Regenerate with NumPy and SoundFile installed:
python Assets/Scripts/Audio/Editor/GenerateBoarSoundConcepts.py
Local dependency fallback: .validation/audio-python (SoundFile only).
WAV output: mono, 48 kHz, PCM16. Upsampling does not restore source bandwidth.
Preview: output/boar-audio/escuchar.html
Integration: Assets/Data/Enemies/ForestCreatures/Boar.asset references all five
approved B clips, shared by Boar_Standard, Boar_Forest_Moss and Boar_Dark_Fur.
Preparation and execution volumes are 0.8; hurt volume is 0.75. Existing
animation timings, damage, travel and cooldowns are preserved.
The approved WAVs and their GUIDs are unchanged by the integration.
BoarAudioIntegration.RunBatch validates current imported settings (without
repairing them), the three prefab dependency sets, a Windows content bundle,
and combat events in Play Mode. Reports: output/boar-audio. A content bundle
check is not a full player build.
