Generado con la herramienta integrada de imágenes. Logo de fantasía voxel con letras de piedra, bordes dorados, musgo y un acento luminoso. No se ha reemplazado el título del menú ni reconstruido la build.

## Prompt final

Use case: logo-brand. Create one polished, beautiful main title logo for an indie voxel fantasy adventure game. Exact only text: "MISMO", spelled M I S M O, immediately legible. Context: charming explorable voxel forests, medieval villages, goblins, day/night skies. Design a custom substantial block-carved wordmark with refined beveled edges, warm ivory stone letter faces, restrained antique-gold edging, deep forest-green dimensional sides and a dark crisp silhouette. A few tasteful moss details integrate into upper edges, suggesting an ancient world waiting to be explored. A tiny faceted warm light accent may be integrated within the O; maintain all five letters fully readable. Premium handcrafted game identity, inviting and adventurous, balanced broad horizontal composition, strong typography dominates, subtle voxel geometry rather than noisy tiny pixels, highly polished illustrative finish, readable at menu-header size. No characters, no busy scenery, no shield, no extra words, no tagline, no watermark. Isolate the complete logo with generous clean padding on a genuinely transparent background (alpha), no backdrop, no checkerboard painted into the art. One final logo only, not a presentation board or multiple variants.
